## KEYWORD STRATEGY TIPS:

✅ DO's:
- Use comma-separated keywords (no spaces after commas)
- Think like a customer searching
- Use both single words and 2-word phrases
- Include your category (shopping, store, grocery)
- Add location-specific terms (indian, local)
- Include action terms (deals, offers, sale)

❌ DON'Ts:
- Don't repeat words from your app name
- Don't use spaces (only commas)
- Don't use special characters
- Don't use brand names (competitors)
- Don't exceed 100 characters
- Don't use plural and singular of same word
